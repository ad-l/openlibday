# GROUP: with the box checked unit clicks add units to the selection (or
# take them out), up to five; the unit last added leads. A group attacks
# together (walking into range first if it must) and moves together onto
# the clicked tile and the free tiles around it, all walks playing at once.
# After a group order the next unit click starts a new selection.
# Run: openlibday --headless --script scripts/group.txt  (needs LIBDAY_ROOT)
ai off
skirmish Opportunity Knocks
key Return
key Return
key Return
deploy Grenadier
tile 44 18
deploy Grenadier
tile 45 18
deploy Grenadier
tile 44 19
key Return
key Y
# A Claptrap in range of two of the Grenadiers.
place 15 40 18
tile 44 18
expect selection [27]
button Group
expect grouping true
tile 45 18
tile 44 19
expect selection [27,28,29]
expect selected 29
# Clicking a member takes it out.
tile 45 18
expect selection [27,29]
tile 45 18
tile 12 19
tile 13 21
expect selection [27,29,28,0,1]
expect selected 1
tile 13 19
expect message Too many selected units
expect selection [27,29,28,0,1]
tile 12 19
tile 13 21
expect selection [27,29,28]
# Group fire: the Grenadier out of range walks up first, then all three
# throw, the leader first.
centre 42 18
tile 40 18
tile 40 18
idle
expect units.28.x 44
expect units.28.y 17
expect units.15.armor 11
expect selection [27,29,28]
# The order went out: a unit click starts afresh.
tile 44 18
expect selection [27]
tile 44 19
tile 44 17
expect selection [27,29,28]
# Group move: all three walk at once to (40, 22) and around it, as far as
# their AP goes.
tile 40 22
tile 40 22
wait 2
expect walking [28,29,27]
idle
expect walking []
expect units.27.x 42
expect units.27.y 20
expect units.29.x 41
expect units.29.y 22
expect units.28.x 43
expect units.28.y 19
# Unchecking GROUP keeps only the leader.
button Group
expect grouping false
expect selection [28]
