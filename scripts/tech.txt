# Technology capture (docs/tech.md): a human unit next to a building with a
# TechReward captures it (bulb over the building, TECH.WAV) and releases it
# when it leaves; the battle report totals the rewards captured at the end.
# Run: openlibday --headless --script scripts/tech.txt  (needs LIBDAY_ROOT
# and the Testing Ground generator installed)
ai off
skirmish Testing Ground
key Return

key Y

expect tech.captured []

wait 1
