# MP6 co-op campaign: a host and a client (in-process copies) start the
# campaign together. Both defend their headquarters in the first attack,
# then each builds their own base part with their own credits and buys
# their own upgrades; the host picks the province to invade and closes the
# reports, and every player gets the rewards.
# Run: openlibday --headless --script scripts/mp_campaign.txt  (needs LIBDAY_ROOT)
